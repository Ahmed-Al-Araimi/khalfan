# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-05)

- A calmer Khalfan: he walks, climbs, hangs, naps, rides his cloud and gets angry. More tricks arrive in later
  updates, a few at a time.
- His rage reaches every window now: while he is angry he burns them one by one, and a window you open before he
  calms down is next. Click a window instead of giving him his coffee, and that one goes first.
- The burn is quicker: a window turns to dust in under a second.
- Still here: pick him up and throw him, call him over, the Flying Nimbus, his angry power-up and window burn, sound,
  mischief and Start with Windows.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-03)

- Khalfan now comes as a Windows installer: download `Khalfan-Setup.exe`, double-click, done. No Git, no Node.js.
- He updates himself: new versions download quietly and he restarts into them.
- Uninstall him from Windows Settings, like any app.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-03)

The first public Khalfan.

- He lives on your taskbar and on top of your windows: walks, jumps, climbs, hangs from edges and naps.
- Follows your mouse; pick him up, swing him and throw him.
- His angry power-up burns one window away (minimized, never closed). Switch it off in the tray.
- The Flying Nimbus, his pixel speech bubbles and sounds.
- Tray menu, Ctrl+Alt+K to show or hide him, Start with Windows.
